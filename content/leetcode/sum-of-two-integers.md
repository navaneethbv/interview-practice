# Sum of Two Integers

Return the sum of integers `a` and `b` without using the `+` or `-` operators.

## Examples

### Example 1

```text
Input: a = 7, b = 5
Output: 12
Explanation: Seven plus five is twelve.
```

### Example 2

```text
Input: a = -6, b = 2
Output: -4
Explanation: Combining -6 and 2 gives -4.
```

## Constraints

- -1,000 <= a, b <= 1,000
