# House Robber

Each entry gives the money stored in a house on a straight street.
You may select houses to rob, but never two neighboring houses.
Return the largest total you can collect.

## Examples

### Example 1

```text
Input: nums = [3, 2, 5, 1]
Output: 8
Explanation: Take the houses containing 3 and 5.
```

### Example 2

```text
Input: nums = [2, 7, 9, 3, 1]
Output: 12
Explanation: Take the houses containing 2, 9, and 1.
```

## Constraints

- 1 <= nums.length <= 100
- 0 <= nums[i] <= 400
