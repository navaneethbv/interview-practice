# Reverse Vowels of a String

Reverse the order of vowels in s while keeping every other character at its original position.
Both uppercase and lowercase a, e, i, o, and u are vowels, and their original case travels with them.

## Examples

### Example 1

```text
Input: s = "hello"
Output: "holle"
Explanation: Swap the two vowels e and o.
```

### Example 2

```text
Input: s = "aA"
Output: "Aa"
Explanation: The uppercase and lowercase vowels exchange positions.
```

## Constraints

- 1 <= s.length <= 300000.
- s contains printable ASCII characters.
