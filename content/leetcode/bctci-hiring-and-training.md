# Hiring And Training

Begin with one trained recruiter and no untrained employees.
On a hiring day, every recruiter hires exactly one employee.
On a training day, every untrained employee becomes a recruiter.
Return the minimum days needed to reach exactly `n` recruiters, never allowing total staff to exceed n.

## Constraints

- 2 <= n <= 10,000.


## Examples

### Example 1

```text
Input: [6]
Output: 5
```

### Example 2

```text
Input: [7]
Output: 7
```
