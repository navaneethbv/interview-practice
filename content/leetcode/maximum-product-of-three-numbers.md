# Maximum Product of Three Numbers

Choose three distinct indices in `nums` and return the largest product of their values.
Negative values can contribute to a positive optimum.

## Constraints

- `3 <= nums.length <= 10000`.
- Values range from -1000 to 1000.

## Examples

### Example 1

```text
Input: nums = [-5, -4, 1, 3]
Output: 60
Explanation: The two negative values multiplied by 3 give 60.
```

### Example 2

```text
Input: nums = [1, 2, 3, 4]
Output: 24
Explanation: Use the largest three positive values.
```
