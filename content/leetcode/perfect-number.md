# Perfect Number

A positive integer is perfect when the sum of its positive divisors smaller than itself equals the integer.
Return whether num is perfect.

## Examples

```text
Input: num = 28
Output: true
Explanation: The proper divisors 1,2,4,7,14 sum to 28.
```

```text
Input: num = 12
Output: false
Explanation: The proper divisors sum to 16 rather than 12.
```

## Constraints

- 1 <= num <= 100,000,000
- Do not include num itself in the divisor sum.
