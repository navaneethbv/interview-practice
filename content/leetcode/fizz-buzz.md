# Fizz Buzz

For integers 1 through n, output FizzBuzz for multiples of both 3 and 5, Fizz for other multiples of 3, Buzz for other multiples of 5, and the decimal number otherwise.

## Examples

### Example 1

```text
Input: n = 5
Output: ["1", "2", "Fizz", "4", "Buzz"]
Explanation: Multiples of 3 and 5 receive their labels.
```

### Example 2

```text
Input: n = 1
Output: ["1"]
Explanation: One is divisible by neither 3 nor 5.
```

## Constraints

- 1 <= n <= 10,000
