# Factorial Trailing Zeroes

Return the number of zeros at the end of the decimal representation of n factorial.
The factorial of zero is 1.
Avoid constructing the enormous factorial value.

## Examples

### Example 1

```text
Input: n = 10
Output: 2
Explanation: 10 factorial ends in two zeros.
```

### Example 2

```text
Input: n = 25
Output: 6
Explanation: Multiples of 5 contribute five factors, and 25 contributes one additional factor of 5.
```

## Constraints

- 0 <= n <= 10000.
