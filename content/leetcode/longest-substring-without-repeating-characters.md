# Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters

Find the length of the longest contiguous part of `s` whose characters are all distinct.
Spaces and punctuation are characters too.

## Constraints

- `0 <= s.length <= 50000`.
- `s` contains English letters, digits, spaces, or symbols.

## Examples

### Example 1

```text
Input: s = "abcaef"
Output: 5
Explanation: The substring bcaef has five distinct characters.
```

### Example 2

```text
Input: s = "zzzz"
Output: 1
Explanation: Any single z is valid.
```
