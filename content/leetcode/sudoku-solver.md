# Sudoku Solver

Fill every . cell of a 9 by 9 Sudoku board in place.
Each row, column, and aligned 3 by 3 box must contain every digit 1 through 9 exactly once.
Do not change given digits.
The puzzle has exactly one solution; the displayed output is the completed board.

## Constraints

- board has exactly 9 rows and 9 columns.
- Each cell is . or a digit from 1 through 9.
