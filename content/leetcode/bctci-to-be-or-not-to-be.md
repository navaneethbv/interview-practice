# To Be or Not to Be

For each word of `sentence`, choose whether to keep it.
Return every resulting sentence, with kept words in their original order separated by single spaces, in any order.
Each choice counts separately, so repeated words can produce repeated sentences.

## Examples

### Example 1

```text
Input: sentence = "I love dogs"
Output: ["", "I", "love", "dogs", "I love", "I dogs", "love dogs", "I love dogs"]
```

### Example 2

```text
Input: sentence = ""
Output: [""]
```

## Constraints

- The sentence has at most 12 words separated by single spaces.
