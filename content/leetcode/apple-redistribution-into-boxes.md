# Apple Redistribution into Boxes

The packages in apple hold apples that may be split freely among boxes.
Each box can hold up to its corresponding capacity.
Return the minimum number of boxes needed to contain every apple.

## Constraints

- Both arrays have lengths from 1 to 50.
- Values range from 1 to 50.
- Total box capacity is sufficient.

## Examples

### Example 1

```text
Input: apple = [2, 3], capacity = [1, 4, 3]
Output: 2
Explanation: The boxes with capacities 4 and 3 hold all five apples.
```

### Example 2

```text
Input: apple = [4], capacity = [5]
Output: 1
Explanation: One box is sufficient.
```
