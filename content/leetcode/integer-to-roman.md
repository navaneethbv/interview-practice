# Integer to Roman

Represent num using Roman numerals.
The symbols I, V, X, L, C, D, and M represent 1, 5, 10, 50, 100, 500, and 1000.
Use the subtractive forms IV, IX, XL, XC, CD, and CM for 4, 9, 40, 90, 400, and 900.

## Examples

### Example 1

```text
Input: num = 44
Output: "XLIV"
Explanation: 40 is XL and 4 is IV.
```

### Example 2

```text
Input: num = 2026
Output: "MMXXVI"
Explanation: Combine 2000, 20, 5, and 1.
```

## Constraints

- 1 <= num <= 3999.
