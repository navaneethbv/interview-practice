# Maximum Nesting Depth of the Parentheses

Return the largest number of simultaneously open parentheses in the valid expression s.
Characters other than parentheses do not affect depth.

## Constraints

- s has 1 to 100 characters and is a valid parentheses expression.
- Other characters are digits or arithmetic symbols.

## Examples

### Example 1

```text
Input: s = "(1+(2*3))"
Output: 2
Explanation: The inner pair is nested inside the outer pair.
```

### Example 2

```text
Input: s = "1+2"
Output: 0
Explanation: No parentheses are present.
```
